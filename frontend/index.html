About PhD NexusCare

NexusCare is a research project focused on making healthcare data management accessible, private, and intelligent. Built with a privacy-first approach, all processing happens locally on your device - no cloud dependencies, no subscriptions, and complete data ownership.

🎓 Research-Backed

Developed as part of PhD research in medical informatics and AI applications in healthcare.

🌍 Open Source

Free and open-source. Contribute, customize, and deploy on your own infrastructure.

🔐 Privacy First

Your data never leaves your device. No tracking, no analytics, no third-party access.
